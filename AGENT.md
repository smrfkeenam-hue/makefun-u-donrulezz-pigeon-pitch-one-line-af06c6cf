# AGENT.md

Entry for "Pigeon Pitch, One Line" (u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf).

The entry is one line, at most 200 characters: a city pigeon pitching
investors on Crumbly, a breadcrumb subscription box delivered by pigeon and
eaten by pigeon — raising a seed round, and yes, the seed is literal.

Run: `python3 main.py` — writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing
newline. Stdlib only (`import os`).
